Ring your iPhone timer from Aurix

When you press play on a timed task in Aurix, on your phone or your PC, your iPhone's Clock app starts the same timer. It rings even if Aurix is closed or your phone is on silent. About 6 minutes, once. Tick each step as you go.

If it doesn't ring

No email arrived

Check the key in Aurix's bell panel. A red message there means the email wasn't sent. Make sure the key came from your iCloud address.

The email went to Junk

Open it in Junk and tap Move to Inbox (or Not Junk), then test again. Automations only see mail in your inbox.

The email arrived but no timer started

In Shortcuts → Automation, open the Email automation and check it says Run Immediately and Subject Contains Aurix timer, spelled exactly.

The timer is the wrong length

Check Start Timer is set to seconds, not minutes, and that its number is Item from List.

Good to know

The iPhone timer starts when the email lands, usually a few seconds late. Pausing or stopping in Aurix can't stop it, so cancel it in the Clock app. Web3Forms is free for about 250 emails a month, and each start or resume uses one.
